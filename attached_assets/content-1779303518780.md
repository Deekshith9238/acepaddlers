### Chikmagalur Adventure Packages

Chikmagalur is a hill station located near the city of Bangalore. Five hours by car from Bangalore can take you to this beautiful place. There are numerous Chikmagalur tour packages that can help you plan your tour to this impressive town. Nature lovers ought to love this place because of its magnificent hills, cascading waterfalls, picturesque sunset, organic farms, coffee plantation hub and many more. Despite being a small traditional area this place has so much to offer and is perfect for a weekend getaway. Light rainfall, pleasant breeze, and chill weather are some of the added bonus. Not only this has much, but this place also offered many adventure sports like rafting and trekking.

You can trek through Mullayanagiri and Baba Budangiri ranges. Both the hills offer beautiful views of sunrises, valleys, and greenery. You can also go trekking through the Kudremukh peak located at a short distance from Chikmagalur. It is one of the highest peaks stretching till 1894 meters above the sea level. There you will come across the beautiful Jhari falls resembling buttermilk falling from a height of 328 feet. Then, there are numerous coffee plantation fields that fall on the way to the hill station. All the Chikmagalur packages can be personalized on the basis of your preference. Thus, there will be numerous options where you can choose. If you love adventure then you can take resort to packages offering trekking, rafting, hiking, etc.

Coorg is surrounded by diverse mountain topography, dense forest, and coffee plantation and is just 250 km away from Bangalore. Two to three days can be enjoyed here.
